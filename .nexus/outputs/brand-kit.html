09 · Brand Introduction (From Official Websites)
Miami Coffee Cart, Miami Matcha Cart, Miami Hot Chocolate Cart, and Miami Refresher Cart are services of Luisa Coffee (https://luisacoffee.com), providing mobile specialty beverage carts and professional baristas for weddings, corporate events, trade shows, brand activations, schools, universities, nonprofits, and private parties across Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach counties. We buy green 100% Arabica beans directly at origin, roast them ourselves in Colombia, and serve them alongside ceremonial-grade matcha, craft refreshers, and rich hot chocolate topped with shaved chocolate and gold flake.
