## Element 07 · Brand Guidelines (Do's & Don'ts Based on Website Standards)

### ✅ 3 Specific Do's
1. **DO** highlight that all three carts (**Miami Coffee Cart**, **Miami Matcha Cart**, and **Miami Hot Chocolate Cart**) are services of **Luisa Coffee** (`https://luisacoffee.com`) and can be paired together at the same event.
2. **DO** state the exact operational requirements clearly: **Standard 120V outlet**, **at least 5×5 ft area**, and **starting price of $700** (2 hours of service, 1 barista, up to 50 guests).
3. **DO** list the exact dairy-free milk alternatives offered across the carts: **oat milk, almond milk, and coconut milk**.

### ❌ 3 Specific Don'ts
1. **DON'T** invent menu items not listed on the websites; stick to the official menus (Espresso, Americano, Cappuccino, Latte, Macchiato, Cortado, Vanilla/Caramel/Hazelnut/Mocha/White Chocolate Mocha Lattes, Iced Coffee, Cold Brew, Nitro Cold Brew, Chai Latte, Iced/Hot/Strawberry Matcha Latte, and Classic/Spiced Hot Chocolate with shaved chocolate and gold flake).
2. **DON'T** omit the parent brand connection to **Luisa Coffee** or the **Luisa Moments** live photo wall & guestbook feature.
3. **DON'T** alter the official contact channels: `(786) 530-2517` (`cart@luisacoffee.com`, `matcha@luisacoffee.com`, `hotchocolate@luisacoffee.com`) and `(786) 659-4164` (`info@luisacoffee.com`).
